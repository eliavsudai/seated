Seated · Wedding Seating Chart

Privacy Policy

Your guest list stays yours
The short version Seated does not collect any data. Your guest list, tables, seating and notes are stored only on your iPhone. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What Seated stores

Everything you enter: your names and wedding details, guests and their RSVPs, meals, dietary or accessibility notes, tables, room layout and seating rules. All of it is kept in a local database on your device. It is included in your device backups (iCloud or Finder) like the data of any other app, under Apple's protection.

What we collect

Nothing. Seated contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it makes no network requests. We, the developers, cannot see your data, and nobody else can either.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to send it. Exporting a PDF, an image or a backup file opens the iOS share sheet, and you decide where it goes: AirDrop, Mail, Messages, Files or a printer. From that point the file is handled by the app or service you picked, under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

Seated asks for no permissions. Importing a CSV or a backup uses the system file picker, which only gives the app the single file you select.

Children

Seated is rated 4+ and collects no information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings › Reset all data in the app, or delete the app. Either way everything is removed from your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
