Questions

How do I add my whole guest list at once?

Go to Guests › + › Paste a list of names, then paste one name per line from Notes, Excel or a text thread. "Tom & Jane Smith" becomes two guests in the same household. You can also import a CSV file (Guests › + › Import a CSV file).

How do I seat someone?

On the Floor plan, drag a name from the tray at the bottom onto a seat. You can also tap the name and then tap a seat. To move someone already seated, touch and hold their seat, then drag. Drop them back on the tray to unseat them.

How do I move a table?

Touch and hold the table top for a moment, then drag. Turn "Snap to grid" on or off with the grid button. Pinch to zoom, and use the frame button to fit the room to the screen.

What do the rose badges mean?

A seating rule is broken (for example, two people you want kept apart are at the same table), or a table has more guests than seats. Tap the rules icon on the Floor plan to see which one.

Will Auto-seat move people I've already placed?

No. Auto-seat only fills empty seats with attending guests who don't have a seat yet. It never moves locked guests or locked tables, and it never breaks a rule. You get a preview with Keep and Undo.

Can my partner work on the plan too?

Yes. Go to Settings › Back up & share plan › Export backup and send the file by AirDrop, Messages or email. On their phone, open Seated and choose Import backup. Your data isn't synced automatically because it never leaves your phones, so agree on one "master copy" and pass the file back and forth.

How do I print the seating chart?

Go to Stationery › Seating chart poster, pick Garden, Modern or Classic and a size (18×24 in, 24×36 in or A2), then tap Save as PDF. The PDF is vector, so any print shop can print it sharply at full size.

Where is my data stored? Is there a subscription?

Only on your iPhone. Seated is a one-time purchase with no subscription, no ads and no account. See the privacy policy.

I got a new phone. How do I move my plan?

Restoring from an iCloud or computer backup brings Seated's data along. You can also export a Seated backup file and import it on the new phone.
